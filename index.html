Scheduling

Automated Meeting Scheduling

One API call becomes a calendar event, a Google Meet link and a polished invitation email.

Trigger

A POST request sends the meeting title and optional start and end times.

Process

Missing times get defaults (now, for two hours). A Calendar event with a Meet link is created.

Result

A branded HTML email with a Join button goes out, and the webhook replies to the caller.

  • n8n Webhook
  • Google Calendar
  • Gmail
  • HTML email

Good for: consultants, coaches and agencies that book many calls.

Customer support

AI Technical Support Bot

AI reads the customer's mood and alerts the team the moment someone is unhappy.

Trigger

A feedback form collects the customer's name, email and message.

AI and logic

A sentiment model scores the message. A code step sets the priority to High, Medium or Low.

Routing

Negative feedback alerts the team on Slack with the customer's details. Every customer gets an email.

  • Form Trigger
  • Hugging Face
  • JavaScript
  • Slack
  • Gmail

Good for: support teams that need to catch unhappy customers fast.

Content

AI LinkedIn Content Creator

Trigger

Publish

  • Schedule Trigger

  • LinkedIn

Good for: founders and freelancers who want a steady LinkedIn presence.
